# Four-minute MarketPilot AI demonstration

Start on the landing page with a fresh workspace. If you have rehearsed in the same tab, use **Restart Demo** in the sidebar, confirm, and choose **Back to website**.

| Time | What to show | What to point out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Choose **Try Demo**. Show Overview, then **Add Product**. | Demo Mode is visible. The dashboard starts with four products and four active listings. |
| 0:25–1:05 | Choose **Use Sample Product** and move through the steps with **Next**. Pause on costs and the review. | The seller identifies a 15 cm mahogany elephant. It costs $8 to make and $3 to ship, has a $20 expected price, and has 20 units available. |
| 1:05–1:45 | Choose **Analyze Product**, inspect the result, then **Find Similar Products**. Select two comparable listings, open **Compare Selected**, and close the comparison. | Seller inputs and editable suggestions are separate. The photos do not verify material or durability. Compare size, material, shipping, and listed prices. |
| 1:45–2:30 | Open **View Market Intelligence**, then **Get Pricing Recommendations**. Show Balanced pricing and change the Etsy price to **$28.99**. | Fictional average asking prices are $26.50 on Etsy and $22.80 on eBay. Balanced starts at $26.99 and $23.99. With the sample costs, the edited Etsy price gives $14.84 estimated profit per unit. |
| 2:30–3:15 | Choose **Generate Listings**. Inspect each marketplace tab, edit the Etsy title, then choose **Review Final Listings**. | Compare the previews. The review carries the edited title and price forward. The seller chooses which marketplaces to approve. |
| 3:15–4:00 | Leave both marketplaces selected. Choose **Confirm and Publish**, then **Publish Demo Listings**. Open **View My Listings** when the receipts appear. | Show the two demo IDs and final prices. Open one listing or pause and resume it. All publication happens inside this tab. |

For another run, open the sidebar and choose **Restart Demo**. **Cancel** preserves the current run. Confirming restores the original data.
